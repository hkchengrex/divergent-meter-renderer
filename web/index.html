How closely will the render match?

Both renderers use the same numeral paths, tube profile, cathode depths, dot position, camera coordinates, and settings. The browser approximates the glow lighting, glass refraction, floor reflections, and bloom. Blender calculates light bouncing through the scene, so highlights and brightness can differ. Samples and seed affect the Blender render only.

Use a small Blender render to check the look before rendering at full resolution. The preview PNG is the browser image; the command produces the Blender version.
